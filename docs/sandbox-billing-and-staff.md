# Private-pilot sandbox billing and staff operations

This capability is a local sandbox implementation. Pilot access remains free. Every subscription and entitlement is restricted to SANDBOX; no checkout return, provider notice, subscription state, refund or dispute can enable production commerce or change the separate per-user USD provider-consumption ledger. There is no live checkout, refund sender, provider reader or automatic top-up.

## Boundaries and storage

Migration `202611030011_sandbox_billing_admin` installs a private forced-RLS schema, installer-owned tables and a NOLOGIN function owner. Provision `lyraseo_billing_owner` (NOLOGIN), `lyraseo_billing_intake`, `lyraseo_billing_reconciler` and `lyraseo_staff_executor` as distinct NOINHERIT, nonprivileged identities with no role membership, ownership or broad product-table privileges. The migration refuses unsafe existing identities; it does not repair them. The existing operator login stays separate. The loopback test runner provisions only absent local fixture identities and creates new uniquely named databases.

Accounts belong to a stable workspace. Versioned merchant/customer/resource/price bindings come from an offline reviewed operator, never request metadata or email. One base slot spans both providers. A confirmed second subscription remains observable as a conflict without another local entitlement. Pending intents retain their slot until a canonical read confirms an effective end; elapsed time alone cannot release it. The intent fingerprint and historical sponsor remain fixed through ownership changes. Every intent also freezes provider, merchant, exact external price/mapping version and a monotonic account generation. A separate immutable admission binding connects that intent to one exact external subscription before entitlement. Confirmed effective ends append permanent retirement evidence; an old resource cannot adopt a newly reserved slot, even under the same provider/merchant.

Verified delivery records, price mappings, monetary facts, transitions and staff receipts reject updates/deletes. Billing inbox state is mutable and fenced separately. Amounts are nonnegative integral minor-unit decimal strings plus explicit currency/exponent; payment/refund/dispute kinds preserve their economic meaning without FX or netting across currencies. Monetary reference uniqueness prevents duplicate delivery IDs from creating another financial fact. A conflicting amount requires reconciliation rather than rewriting history. Refund totals cannot exceed a known original same-currency payment. A refund does not cancel a subscription or credit an AI usage budget. PENDING, FAILED and SUCCEEDED economic observations are separate immutable facts; only successful effects enter monetary totals. Entitlement requires a successful same-resource, same-period payment reference in the exact frozen currency/exponent, net of successful refunds, meeting the frozen minor-unit amount. A `paid` boolean alone is ignored. The declared net-paid threshold is a synthetic rehearsal policy, not an approved commercial refund or access policy.

The canonical reader interface consumes a fixed persisted sandbox binding. Its network-free fixture implementation is supplied by tests. A missing reader records UNAVAILABLE with bounded backoff; reads time out after ten seconds and never start a purchase. New notices invalidate an in-flight generation. Every completion rechecks the lease after workspace/account/slot/subscription locks. Tombstones withdraw entitlement while minimal obligations remain attributable. Canonical snapshots carry exact source identity, external price/mapping version and a comparable version. Only the explicitly synthetic comparable-version contract is currently qualified for fixtures. Older versions and conflicting ties are retained as rejection evidence and cannot replace newer projections. Missing/unqualified ordering stays in visible reconciliation. Real provider version semantics remain unqualified. There is no timer in a Next request.

## Raw delivery verification

`verifySandboxBillingRequest` is a raw Node adapter retaining `IncomingMessage.rawHeaders`. It admits only the exact two POST paths, rejects cookies/bearer/internal headers, duplicate headers, compression, invalid UTF-8 and streams over 256 KiB independent of Content-Length. It passes original bytes to signature verification and persists verified normalized receipts before acknowledgement.

Razorpay uses a pinned sandbox endpoint secret and signed account identity, HMAC-SHA256 with constant-time decoded-byte comparison. Its event-ID header is an untrusted delivery hint. Same-ID/different-hash deliveries remain collision evidence and still prompt a canonical read. Polar uses pinned official `@polar-sh/sdk@1.0.2`, explicitly selected API `2026-04`, untouched bytes, Standard Webhooks transport ID/timestamp/signatures and pinned organization. Rotation permits a bounded previous secret; exceptions, signatures, payment details and raw payloads are not retained. The SDK is never instantiated as a network client. Event object IDs remain their original payment/order/refund/dispute IDs; signed subscription and payment/order references are normalized separately. Verified supported deliveries are stored before acknowledgement even when unresolved. A separate bounded discovery queue routes only signed references or previously corroborated same-merchant payment references to a matching account/subscription. Refund-before-payment notices can route after the payment evidence arrives. Unknown references or missing organization evidence remain durable UNAVAILABLE/needs-reader states; no caller may choose an arbitrary account.

Stock Next Request loses duplicate-header evidence. Consequently **no proxy exception or hosted webhook route enables this adapter**. Hosted raw ingress, endpoint/account bindings, real signatures, clocks, account eligibility and canonical readers require independent qualification and authorization. A synthetic signature is not a captured provider signature. Official references: [Razorpay validation](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/validate-test), [Razorpay delivery](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/best-practices), [Polar SDK](https://github.com/polarsource/polar/tree/main/sdk/typescript), [Polar sandbox](https://polar.sh/docs/integrate/sandbox).

## Staff admission and controls

`/admin` and every `/api/admin` read/mutation independently resolve the current verified interactive browser actor and require enabled StaffGrant plus verified MFA. The separate `LYRA_STAFF_ADMIN_ENABLED` and `LYRA_SANDBOX_BILLING_ENABLED` flags default off and must remain off until their migrations/read paths pass. The restricted `LYRA_STAFF_DATABASE_URL` must use `lyraseo_staff_executor`. It is a server secret and must never be included in browser configuration. No default staff user, email promotion, bearer credential, customer role or service Actor grants staff access. Named mutation capabilities are separately provisioned offline and audited. The migration records a baseline epoch for existing grants; later staff/capability changes advance authority epochs and invalidate old tokens/intents even after re-enabling a grant.

All changes require same-origin POST, strict schemas, current security/session state, authentication and MFA within ten minutes, a reason, an opaque hashed one-use step-up token bound to the actor/session/epoch/action/target, and an expected revision where applicable. Exact retries return the original result; changed request bytes conflict. Staff reads use fixed bounded projections/cursors and exclude raw content, URLs, credentials and provider payloads. Read/action/denial and offline provisioning receipts are appended separately. Read and step-up rates are bounded per staff member.

Support search requires a separate `support:search` capability plus fresh authentication/MFA, at least8 ASCII identifier characters for exact matching or12 for a literal prefix, and at most25 rows. Indexed C-collation bounds and literal-prefix equality never interpret underscores or percent signs as patterns. Results contain only opaque user/workspace/site IDs, scope IDs and safe suspension/verification/tombstone flags. There is no name/email/domain/provider-subject/content lookup. Per-staff search admission is bounded to ten successful searches per minute; cursor pagination preserves all matches beyond the first25. Search terms are not retained in staff receipts.

The optional `privacy:read` projection independently requires fresh staff authority. When migration32's fixed private helper is absent, the view explicitly reports unavailable. When present, migration33 grants helper EXECUTE only to its validated NOLOGIN definer; the staff login itself cannot call it. The helper returns current local case/tombstone/deadline/task status, without actor or journal/receipt secrets. Pending processor/backup adapters and `allCopiesErased=false` stay visible. The combined test admits a genuine deletion through migration32's public service and filesystem journal rather than inserting a fabricated case.

Views show actual kernel policies, sandbox accounts and reconciliation, operation lease/acceptance state, exact provider budget balances, recovery rehearsal cases and staff receipts. Customer `/app/billing?workspaceId=…` is owner-scoped and read-only, with explicit sandbox/free-pilot labels. Individual staff job retry/cancel remains disabled with an explicit reason. Existing operator APIs support only policy CAS; the signed recovery API processes a global bounded due batch and cannot bind a single requested target. Customer cancellation uses the initiating customer's current Actor, while audit cancellation is runtime-session guarded. Reusing either as support authority would require impersonation or a new target-specific kernel action. Neither is introduced here. Arbitrary balance/refund changes remain unavailable.

Queued staff pause execution is **UNAVAILABLE**. Cached ProductSession rows do not prove current Supabase revocation or `not_after`. The reviewed packet therefore removes kernel policy-write authority from the billing definer and never calls the kernel policy CAS from `executeStaffPause`. The UI disables this control and explains why. No new session issuer, stored provider secret, fabricated Actor or asserted synchronization is introduced. A qualified same-request provider-session revalidation protocol, with post-lock authority checks, remains required before this bridge can execute. Independent existing kernel operator policy administration is not represented as a verified browser-staff flow.

The browser uses a durable nonsecret request ID for immediate staff metadata changes. `prepare_request` freezes actor/session/security/action/target/args/reason and a server-only step-up hash; `execute_request` consumes or replays that same request under current verified Node actor/MFA and database authority checks. The browser retains only frozen nonsecret metadata under a stable principal identifier, never a step-up, provider, DB or login token. A bounded server pending projection also reveals unresolved requests after browser storage loss or session replacement. A different request is blocked until the original is resolved. The same currently authorized principal may inspect a prior session's result, but cannot execute its old authorization. Confirmed unconsumed expired or rejected requests can be safely abandoned under the same locks as execution, with an audit receipt. Committed requests require explicit acknowledgement of the observed result digest; a lost acknowledgement can be retried safely. Current named capability, account security state and fresh MFA are required for resolution; another principal cannot inspect or acknowledge the request. Legacy `step-up`/`action` clients retain their signatures, but their mutation now creates or replays the same durable lifecycle using a v4 UUID allocated once and bound to the unique full token hash. The database's shared principal lock blocks fresh legacy tokens or different actions while any request remains unresolved. A committed legacy result includes its nonsecret request ID and digest for acknowledgement; repeating its same token/payload returns the original result. An uncommitted legacy action returns HTTP 409 with its request ID, lifecycle state and whether it can be abandoned. Old clients that never acknowledge must resolve or upgrade before starting another mutation. The new console never receives a legacy token.

## Recovery rehearsal

Recovery cases use VERIFYING → HOLD → APPROVED or DENIED. The HOLD starts at accepted independent-evidence and prior verified-channel notice receipt hashes and lasts at least 72 hours. The approving staff member must differ from the staff member who accepted the evidence. A current expected revision and fresh step-up are mandatory for transitions. Case creation does not freeze access, transfer ownership or change login identity.

The interface and results explicitly label this LOCAL_REHEARSAL. No notice is actually sent, identity evidence is not collected, and no provider factor is reset. COMPLETED is structurally unavailable until an independently qualified provider workflow can prove supported factor replacement, session/API-token revocation, restricted re-enrollment and completion notices. Approval alone never creates an MFA bypass or a period of normal AAL1 access.
