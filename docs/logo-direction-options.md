## Identity

Use the exact spelling **LyraSEO AI**, Manrope 800 lettering with optical spacing, cobalt #2449D8 and graphite #101828. The compact L has a substantial vertical stem, broad foot and slightly softened outer corners. The wordmark is outlined in the SVGs; it requires no installed font or external font request. The design is independent of LyraShield.

Embed **AEO** vertically inside the L’s upright stroke and **GEO** horizontally inside its foot. The lettering is knocked out of the symbol, showing the surface beneath it, with outlined Manrope 800 glyphs. There is no descriptor beneath LyraSEO AI. AEO means answer engine optimization; GEO means generative engine optimization. The embedded lettering identifies the product category and makes no ranking or inclusion guarantee.

Balanced refinement requested by the founder: AEO/GEO uses 14.25-unit Manrope 800 lettering, midway between the earlier 11.5-unit and enlarged 17-unit versions. The L uses a 24-unit stem and 23.5-unit foot; tile icons use a 44-unit symbol, midway between 40 and 48. Every variant retains the embedded lettering.

## Usage

| Placement | Asset | Minimum display width |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landing hero, report cover, larger brand placements | Wordmark with AEO/GEO inside the L | 600px |
| App header/sidebar and compact marketing navigation | Wordmark with AEO/GEO inside the L | 160px |
| Favicon, collapsed navigation, small app icons | L with AEO/GEO, including the white-on-cobalt tile | 16px |
| Cobalt or other dark solid brand surface | White monochrome variant | Same minimum as its layout |
| Dark app surface | Lighter blue #6C8CFF symbol and #F2F4F7 lettering | Same minimum as its layout |

Keep at least one-quarter of the symbol height clear around the entire lockup. Scale uniformly; do not stretch, add shadows/gradients or rearrange individual letters. Retain AEO/GEO inside every L symbol at every size. For comfortable reading of the embedded lettering, prefer a 600px-wide lockup or a standalone symbol at least 72px high where layout permits. At small icon sizes the lettering remains included but may not be legible; do not substitute a plain L. Logo text stays LyraSEO AI; AEO/GEO is not part of the account/product name. In the eventual UI, provide an accessible brand name for linked logos and avoid duplicate announcement of decorative copies.
